摘要：为了有效降低评吸员个体差异对感官评价结果的干扰，评估其在不同感官评价指标上的感知
敏锐度，以18名评吸员对不同卷烟产品开展的感官评价数据为研究对象，从评价方向偏差和梯度偏
差两个关键维度构建了一种卷烟感官评价指标的敏感性表征方法，并借助熵权法实现参数的客观
赋权及敏感性表征。结果表明：①结合多轮次和多组别的卷烟产品感官评价得分分布特征，筛选得
到4项关键感官评价指标，分别为香气、杂气、刺激性和余味。②不同评吸员对卷烟感官评价指标感
知的敏感性存在显著差异。③所采用的感官性能参数设计方法能够更为准确地识别感知能力较低
的异常评吸员，对提升评价数据的可信度效果表现最优。该研究结果可为评吸员的筛选及评价小
组的组建提供一定的技术支撑。

Panel member’s sensitivity to cigarette sensory evaluation indicators:
construction, characterization and contrastive analysis
YANG Jiadong, YANG Lu, LIU Jihui, SU Li, LIU Zhuoxin, ZHU Feng, QI Lin
*
HongyunHonghe Tobacco (Group) Co., Ltd., Kunming 650231, China
Abstract：To effectively decrease the interference by individual differences among panel assessors on
sensory evaluation results and to evaluate their perceptual sensitivity to key sensory evaluation indicators,
sensory evaluation data from 18 trained assessors on different cigarette products were used as the research
sample. A characterization method for the sensitivity to cigarette sensory evaluation indicators was
developed through two key dimensions: directional bias and gradient deviation in smoking assessments,
and the entropy weight method was applied to objectively weigh the parameters and characterize the
sensitivity. The results showed that: 1) Four key sensory evaluation indicators, namely aroma, offensive
odor, irritation and aftertaste were identified based on the score distribution patterns across multiple rounds
of sensory evaluation on different cigarette products. 2) Significant variations existed in the assessors'
sensitivity to the cigarette sensory indicators. 3) The adopted design method for sensory performance
indicators could effectively identify assessors with low sensory ability, demonstrating an optimal
effectiveness in enhancing the reliability of the evaluation data. This research provides the technical
support for cigarette smoking sensory assessor selection and sensory panel formation.
Keywords：Cigarette; Sensory evaluation; Individual difference; Sensitivity characterization; Sensory
performance parameter; Entropy weight method

3
结论
①结合多轮次和多组别的卷烟产品感官评价得
分分布特征，筛选得到4项关键感官评价指标，分别
为香气、杂气、刺激性和余味。②根据敏感性表征方
法实现了18名评吸员在感官评价指标上感知能力差
异的分析，结果表明不同评吸员在不同感官评价指
标间存在显著的感知敏锐度差异。③与方差分析及
Friedman 检验两种在感官研究领域常用的方法相
比，所采用的感官性能参数设计方法能够更为准确
地识别感知能力较低的异常评吸员，对提升所得评
价数据的可信度效果表现最优。该方法能够对评吸
员的感官性能表现进行科学评估及动态追踪，不仅
为卷烟感官评价组织工作中评吸员的筛选、调整乃
至淘汰环节提供科学依据，还能够提升卷烟感官评
价数据的可靠性及准确性。
