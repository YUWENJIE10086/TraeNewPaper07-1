Research on the construction and application of an MLP-dominated cigarette sales forecasting
model
CUI Jianhua, CHEN Hao, LIU Dongrong*
Zhejiang Tobacco Monopoly Administration, Hangzhou 310001, China
Abstract: Cigarette market sales exhibit strong cyclical, seasonal, and strategic characteristics, making it difficult for traditional forecasting
models to capture the impact of key non-market factors such as policy directives, supply plans, delivery rhythms, inventory balance, and
monopoly regulations. To address this, this paper combines a Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) with a Large Language Model (LLM) and
proposes a combined forecasting model for cigarette sales characterized by “MLP dominance and LLM assistance”. In this framework, the
MLP excels at modeling natural data patterns, while the LLM provides capabilities for multi-source information understanding and
reasoning. Based on this forecasting model, the study further applies it to cigarette inventory transfer strategies and proposes a dynamic
optimal control method for cigarette logistics supply chain inventory based on hierarchical Distributed Model Predictive Control (DMPC).
Using real historical data from Province Z covering the 18-month period from January 2022 to June 2023, the sales forecasting and
inventory control are evaluated. Results show that the proposed forecasting model achieves a mean relative prediction error of 3.5% for
fast-selling stock-keeping units, significantly outperforming ARIMA (20.0%) and the RBF neural network (8.3%). Furthermore, the
proposed inventory control method outperforms the traditional inventory-to-sales ratio approach across three cigarette inventory transfer
indicators (average inventory, number of transport trips, and standard deviation of inbound volume) in a front warehouse of a
prefecture-level city during different periods, achieving average reductions of 24.21%, 36.84%, and 9.93%, respectively.
Keywords: cigarette logistics; sales forecasting; inventory control; Multi-Layer Perceptron; Large Language Model; model predictive
control
